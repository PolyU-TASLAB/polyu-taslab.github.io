01 / Workshop overview

From capable AI
to dependable robots.

How can embodied AI remain robust, transparent, accountable and safety-assured in open, dynamic environments shared with people?

Robots are moving from laboratory demonstrations into safety-critical, human-centred environments. Foundation models, vision-language-action models and world models are expanding what they can perceive, reason about and do. Real-world deployment still faces distribution shift, sensor degradation, uncertain human intent and the gap between simulation and reality.

This workshop brings together researchers in robot learning and foundation models with experts in perception, navigation, control, human-robot interaction, surgical robotics and AI safety. We will discuss how robots can quantify uncertainty, detect failures, preserve human oversight and transition safely when confidence is insufficient.

Aligned with the IEEE ROBIO 2026 theme, “Biomimetic Robots Empowered by AI,” the programme connects shared trustworthiness challenges across aerial, ground, humanoid and surgical robotics.

02 / Topics of interest

Four themes.
One shared challenge.

Making embodied intelligence dependable
where its decisions have physical consequences.

01

Foundation models
for embodied AI

Foundation models, VLA models and world models for embodied perception, spatial intelligence and multimodal reasoning under uncertainty.

02

Trustworthy
embodied autonomy

Robustness, uncertainty quantification, transparency, failure detection, integrity monitoring, safe fallback and safety certification.

03

Human-centred
intelligence

Human intent modelling, language-conditioned interaction, shared control, human-robot collaboration and accountable human oversight.

04

Deployment
and evaluation

UAVs and aerial manipulation, autonomous vehicles, humanoid and legged robots, and robot-assisted surgery. Sim-to-real transfer, benchmarks and datasets.

Cross-cutting topic: robust multi-sensor state estimation, localisation and navigation in degraded and out-of-distribution environments.
