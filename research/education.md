## Courses and Teaching Platforms

<ul class="rt-list">
  <li><b>AAE4011 Artificial Intelligence in Unmanned Autonomous Systems</b> — AI for drones and ground robots: perception, planning and decision-making with deep and reinforcement learning.</li>
  <li><b>AAE5306 Electronics Design and Informatics for the Low-Altitude Economy</b> — electronics, sensing and AI tools taught through drone applications.</li>
  <li><b>AAE4203 Guidance and Navigation</b> — GNSS positioning (SPP, DGNSS, RTK), visual navigation, Kalman filtering and factor graph optimisation, with video lectures and tutorials.</li>
  <li><b>AAE3004 Dynamical Systems and Control</b> — modelling, stability and feedback control for aerospace and robotics.</li>
  <li><b>AAE6102 Satellite Communication and Navigation</b> (invited lectures) — advanced GNSS, multi-sensor integration and AI-aided urban navigation.</li>
</ul>

## Robotics Competitions

<ul class="rt-list">
  <li><b>PolyU AI &amp; Robotics Club</b> — we support students from many disciplines in designing, building and programming robots for national and international competitions.</li>
  <li><b>DJI RoboMaster and ABU Robocon</b> — mentoring student teams in autonomous robot competitions with AI perception and real-time strategy.</li>
  <li><b>ICRA robot challenges</b> — autonomous navigation, manipulation and multi-robot coordination tasks.</li>
  <li><b>UAV competitions</b> — indoor navigation, aerial manipulation and drone swarms.</li>
  <li><b>Interdisciplinary training</b> — mechanical design, embedded systems, computer vision, SLAM and reinforcement learning.</li>
</ul>
