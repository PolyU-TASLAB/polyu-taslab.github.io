## PolyU and CUHK-Shenzhen Successfully Co-host Joint Workshop on AI and Intelligent Robotics

The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU) and The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen) successfully co-hosted a Joint Workshop on AI and Intelligent Robotics on 22 August 2026 at CUHK-Shenzhen. The workshop brought together faculty members and students from both universities for an afternoon of research presentations, technical discussions, and laboratory exchange.

Prof. Fangxun Zhong of CUHK-Shenzhen and Prof. Weisong Wen of PolyU opened the programme and welcomed participants. Across two presentation sessions, researchers from the two universities shared work spanning safe path planning and control, endoscopic 3D perception, embodied navigation, surgical robotics, GNSS/IMU smoothing, vision-guided robotic systems, LiDAR/IMU/UWB integration, language-guided robot perception, infrastructure-light localization, robot hand-eye calibration, roadside-GNSS-aided vehicle positioning, AI-assisted clinical guidance, and radio-aided SLAM.

The compact presentation format combined focused talks with question-and-answer sessions, encouraging participants to compare methods, discuss practical challenges, and identify connections between research in trustworthy autonomous systems and intelligent robotics. A group discussion further explored promising research directions and opportunities for continued academic exchange.
