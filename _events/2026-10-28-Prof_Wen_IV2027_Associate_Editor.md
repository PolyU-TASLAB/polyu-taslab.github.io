Professor Weisong Wen, Director of the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), has been invited to serve as an **Associate Editor (Contributed Papers)** on the International Program Committee of the **[2027 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV 2027)](https://ieee-iv.org/2027/)**.

Sponsored by the [IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society (ITSS)](https://ieee-itss.org/), the IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium is one of the leading international conferences on intelligent and autonomous vehicles. IV 2027 will be held on **15–18 June 2027** in **[Perth, Australia](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Perth+WA,+Australia)**, with Prof. Thomas Bräunl (The University of Western Australia) as General Chair, and Prof. Ulrich Rückert (Bielefeld University) and Prof. Sébastien Glaser (Queensland University of Technology) as Program Co-Chairs.

As an Associate Editor, Prof. Wen will help manage the peer review of submitted papers, including assigning reviewers and making recommendations for the symposium proceedings. The role adds to his editorial service as Associate Editor of the [IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles](https://ieee-itss.org/pub/t-iv/).

The submission deadline for contributed papers is **15 November 2026**. Researchers working on intelligent vehicles, autonomous driving, perception, localization and navigation are encouraged to submit their work. Submission and review guidelines are available on the [IV 2027 website](https://ieee-iv.org/2027/contributions/reviews-guidelines/).
