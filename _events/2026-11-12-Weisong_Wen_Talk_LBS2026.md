On 12 November 2026, **Prof. Weisong Wen** of the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB), The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, delivered a talk at **LBS 2026, the 20th International Conference on Location Based Services**, in Nanjing, China. His presentation, titled *AI-Assisted Integrity Monitoring for Multi-Sensor Fusion Positioning in Complex Environments*, focused on trustworthy positioning and navigation for unmanned systems.

### About the presentation

The talk addressed AI-assisted integrity monitoring for multi-sensor fusion positioning in complex environments, connecting integrity concepts from civil aviation with trustworthy navigation of unmanned systems.

The presentation took place from **14:40 to 15:00 (UTC+8)** in **Session S02: Crowdsensing and Location Intelligence**, held in the Knowledge Room on Floor 5. The session was chaired by **Prof. Yunbin Yuan** and **Prof. Shengfeng Gu** and brought together presentations on localization, GNSS positioning, navigation and location intelligence.

### About LBS 2026

LBS 2026 takes place on **11-13 November 2026 in Nanjing, China**, hosted by **Southeast University** and the **Chinese Academy of Surveying and Mapping**. The conference provides a forum for research on location-based services and related positioning and navigation technologies.
