On 1 September 2026, members of the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) supported the PolyU FENG AI & Robotics Club during PolyU Student Orientation 2026. The event welcomed new students to campus and gave them an opportunity to discover the club's activities, meet its members and explore how artificial intelligence and robotics can be applied through hands-on engineering projects.

### Introducing robotics to new students

At the orientation showcase, the club presented a range of robotic platforms and student projects, including humanoid and quadruped robots, mobile robots and competition robots. Live demonstrations and project videos gave visitors a direct view of the club's technical interests and an accessible starting point for students curious about robotics, whether or not they had previous experience.

Prof. Weisong Wen delivered an information session introducing the PolyU FENG AI & Robotics Club and its vision. He also shared examples of TASLAB's research and explained how students can deepen their understanding of intelligent systems through technical exchange, teamwork and practical projects. The session helped prospective members learn more about the club and how to take part in its future activities.

### Engagement across the PolyU community

The booth attracted interest throughout the orientation showcase. The President of PolyU visited the booth, learned about the robots on display and joined the student team for a group photograph. The visit gave club members an encouraging opportunity to present their work and enthusiasm for robotics to the University leadership.

Students also visited the booth and asked about the club's projects and the robotic systems on display. Their interest reflected the wider student community's enthusiasm for AI and robotics and gave the team another opportunity to introduce its technical work and future activities.

The orientation marked an active start to the new academic year for the PolyU FENG AI & Robotics Club. By connecting students with research and practical engineering, the event reflected the club's aim of building an inclusive community for learning, experimentation and collaboration. TASLAB will continue to provide academic and technical guidance as the club develops its activities and supports students interested in AI and robotics.
