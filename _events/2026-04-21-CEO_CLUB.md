On 21 April 2026, Dr. Runqiu Yang and Mr. Junzhe Wang of the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) showcased a robotic dog at the Inauguration of the 10th Executive Committee of the PolyU CEO Club.

At the event, they presented The Hong Kong Polytechnic University's (PolyU) latest advances in intelligent robotics and discussed with business leaders and guests the robot's potential applications in inspection, assistive operations, emergency response and other real-world scenarios. The showcase highlighted the University's commitment to innovation, knowledge transfer and industry collaboration.
