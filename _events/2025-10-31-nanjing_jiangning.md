Officials from the Nanjing Jiangning Economic Development Zone visited The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), where they were introduced to the research of the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB). The delegation was given an overview of several projects, including cleaning drones, tunnel inspection drones, humanoid robots and end-to-end autonomous driving systems.

The exchange underscored the importance of research collaboration and its role in driving industrial and technological development.
