We are pleased to share that the paper "**Edge-AI Driven Low-Altitude Security: A Robust UAV Tracking System With Multi-Scale Attention And Adaptive Motion Modeling**" was accepted for publication in a special section of the *IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology (TVT)* on 10 October 2026.

</div>
<p class="news-caption">The paper has been accepted for publication in a special section of IEEE TVT.</p>

As indicated by its title, the paper focuses on an Edge-AI-driven UAV tracking system for low-altitude security applications. It combines multi-scale attention with adaptive motion modelling to address robust target tracking in low-altitude environments.

This work contributes to ongoing research on intelligent perception and security for low-altitude autonomous systems. Congratulations to all authors and collaborators!
