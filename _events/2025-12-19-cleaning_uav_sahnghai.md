Today, the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) team conducted a field demonstration of automated drone building cleaning at the Shanghai Pudong Software Park in China. The demonstration was a key part of the launch ceremony for the Shanghai Pudong Software Park Low-Altitude Economy Service Platform.

The demonstration received significant attention and support from the Shanghai Government, the Pudong New Area Government and the Aircraft Owners and Pilots Association of China (AOPA-China).

### Industry breakthrough and market potential

Shanghai is characterised by a dense skyline and a very large demand for building cleaning services, so conducting a demonstration there marks a significant breakthrough for the team in drone-based cleaning. This milestone validates the maturity of the technology and shows the solution's potential for application in the complex environments of mega-cities.
