On 3 July 2025, Nano Coating Tech and DRONE SOLUTIONS ASIA visited the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU) in Hong Kong. The visit aimed to explore collaboration opportunities and facilitate technical exchange in UAV technology and nano-tech applications.

Upon arrival, the visiting team held in-depth discussions with TASLAB members on topics including drone cleaning applications and the potential role of nano-coating technology in building maintenance. The visit also included outdoor testing of nano-material performance, showing its capabilities in real-world conditions.

The visit concluded with a guided tour of TASLAB's facilities, including the UAV flight zone, the navigation testing platform and the autonomous systems testing platform. The exchange laid a solid foundation for upcoming collaboration between PolyU, Nano Coating Tech and DRONE SOLUTIONS ASIA.
