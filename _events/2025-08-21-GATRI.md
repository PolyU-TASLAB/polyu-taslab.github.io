On 21 August 2025 in Guangzhou, the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB), the Guangzhou Aerospace Science and Technology Research Institute (GASTI) and Sunwah InnoHub held an in-depth exchange forum. The parties held extensive discussions on industry-academia-research collaboration in fields such as the low-altitude economy (LAE) and drone technology.

Dr. Wen gave the GASTI delegation a detailed introduction to TASLAB's latest research progress in autonomous systems. The discussion focused on advanced autonomous drone technology, navigation systems for GPS-denied environments and the potential applications of these technologies.

### Summary of discussion

- **Advanced autonomous systems:** TASLAB presented its expertise in developing autonomous drones and advanced navigation systems that operate reliably in complex, GPS-denied environments, highlighting the lab's ability to create highly specialised hardware and software for specific tasks. GASTI recognised the broad application prospects of this technology in building maintenance, railway inspection, logistics delivery and other scenarios.
- **Future development planning:** With the rapid development of the LAE in the Greater Bay Area and related policy support, this technical exchange laid the foundation for deep cooperation between both parties in the industrialisation of autonomous systems technology. Both sides will continue to strengthen technical integration and industrial collaboration, promote the early implementation of relevant cooperation projects, and contribute technical strength to the development of the regional LAE and intelligent manufacturing.
