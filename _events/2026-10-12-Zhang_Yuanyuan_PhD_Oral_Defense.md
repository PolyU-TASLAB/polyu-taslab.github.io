On 12 October 2026, TASLAB PhD student **ZHANG Yuanyuan** successfully passed her PhD oral defense at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Her dissertation, supervised by **Prof. Weisong Wen** and co-supervised by **Prof. Li-Ta Hsu**, is titled *Trustworthy Learning-enabled Safe and Robust Autonomous Navigation for Uncertain Environments*.

Her doctoral research develops trustworthy learning-enabled navigation methods for autonomous systems operating in uncertain, partially observable and dynamic environments. The dissertation brings together four complementary directions:

- **predictive safety assurance**, enabling safety evaluation before an action is executed;
- **epistemic integrity risk monitoring**, improving trust in information under multi-source uncertainty;
- **adaptive robust safe policy optimisation**, addressing changing uncertainty and tail risks; and
- **memory-enhanced safe exploration**, using structured risk memory to learn more safely when risk feedback is sparse or delayed.

These contributions span UAV flight interaction and autonomous ground-robot navigation, connecting safety certification, observation-level trust monitoring, uncertainty-aware reinforcement learning and robust real-world deployment.
