On 12 October 2026, TASLAB PhD student **ZHANG Yuanyuan** successfully passed her PhD oral defense at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Her dissertation, supervised by **Prof. Weisong Wen** and co-supervised by **Prof. Li-Ta Hsu**, is titled *Trustworthy Learning-enabled Safe and Robust Autonomous Navigation for Uncertain Environments*.

Her doctoral research develops trustworthy learning-enabled navigation methods for autonomous systems operating in uncertain, partially observable and dynamic environments. The dissertation brings together four complementary directions:

- **predictive safety assurance**, enabling safety evaluation before an action is executed;
- **epistemic integrity risk monitoring**, improving trust in information under multi-source uncertainty;
- **adaptive robust safe policy optimisation**, addressing changing uncertainty and tail risks; and
- **memory-enhanced safe exploration**, using structured risk memory to learn more safely when risk feedback is sparse or delayed.

These contributions span UAV flight interaction and autonomous ground-robot navigation, connecting safety certification, observation-level trust monitoring, uncertainty-aware reinforcement learning and robust real-world deployment.

The oral examination was conducted by a Board of Examiners comprising:

- **Prof. Wei Liu**, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University — Chair of the Board of Examiners;
- **Prof. Dezong Zhao**, University of Glasgow — External Examiner;
- **Prof. Haocai Huang**, Zhejiang University — External Examiner; and
- **Prof. Weisong Wen**, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University — Chief Supervisor.

We sincerely thank all members of the Board for their time, their careful review of the thesis and their insightful questions and suggestions during the defense.

We would also like to give special thanks to **Dr. WANG Yingying**, postdoctoral fellow at TASLAB, who provided close day-to-day supervision and generous support to Yuanyuan throughout her PhD journey—from shaping research ideas and refining experiments to polishing papers and preparing for the defense. Dr. Wang co-authored five of the six publications arising from the thesis, including papers in *IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems*, *IEEE Internet of Things Journal* and *IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering*. Her guidance played an important part in Yuanyuan's growth as an independent researcher.

TASLAB warmly congratulates Yuanyuan on this important milestone and wishes her every success in the next stage of her career!
