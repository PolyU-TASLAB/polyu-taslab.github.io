On 15 May 2026, members of the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB), Dr. Huang Feng, Mr. Zhang Ziqi and Mr. Qin Qijun, visited the Hong Kong Science Park Shenzhen Branch (HKSTPSZ) and met ASTRI members for an on-site technology exchange.
