**Hong Kong – 12 June 2025** – On the morning of 12 June 2025, members of the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) visited Gammon Construction. The group included Prof. WEN Weisong, Dr. HUANG Feng, PhD students XIAO Naigui and CHEN Yingming, and research assistant WANG Xiangru. The purpose of the visit was to introduce TASLAB's research to Gammon and discuss potential applications in the construction industry.

During the meeting, Prof. Wen presented TASLAB's research fields, past projects and experience. He also demonstrated prototypes of the lab's UAVs and a handheld mapping system, which generated a lot of interest and discussion. Questions included the technical differences between TASLAB's UAVs and commercial DJI drones, and the working principles of the handheld mapping system.

The Gammon team also introduced their expertise and the technologies they use in construction, such as digital twin applications for improving site safety and efficiency.

The meeting served as a practical exchange of ideas and opened up possibilities for future cooperation between the lab and the company.
