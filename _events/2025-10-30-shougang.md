Representatives from the Shougang Jinggang Innovation Center visited The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), where they were introduced to the research of the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB).

<p class="news-caption">The delegation viewed the cleaning drone and the autonomous driving platform during the visit.</p>

The delegation expressed strong appreciation for TASLAB's work and innovations. They commended the lab's dedication to developing safe, reliable and ethically sound autonomous systems, noting that this mission aligns closely with the future direction of technology development.

The representatives were particularly impressed by the exhibited robots, namely the humanoid robot, the cleaning drone and the V2X cooperative autonomous driving platform, which they cited as strong demonstrations of the team's technical capabilities. To further inspire innovation and foster collaboration, the Center invited TASLAB to display these robotic systems in a dedicated exhibition space within the Shougang Jinggang Innovation Center. The partnership is expected to be a valuable opportunity to present the lab's research achievements to a broader audience of industry leaders and potential investors.
