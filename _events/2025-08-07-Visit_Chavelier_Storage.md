On 15 July 2025, a delegation from MTR Corporation, consisting of Ir. Prof. Lawrence Poon, Mr. Kody Lee and Mr. Henry Cheung, met with researchers at the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) in Hong Kong to explore potential technology partnerships.

Dr. Wen presented the lab's latest research in autonomous systems. The discussion focused on how these technologies could be applied to enhance operational efficiency and infrastructure maintenance. Both parties explored pathways for future collaboration, aiming to use the lab's research capabilities to address real-world industrial challenges.

### Summary of discussion

- **Advanced autonomous systems:** TASLAB showcased its expertise in developing autonomous drones and advanced navigation systems designed to operate reliably in complex, GPS-denied environments. The lab's ability to create highly specialised hardware and software for specific tasks was highlighted.
- **Partnership framework:** The conversation centred on establishing a collaborative framework. Key topics included aligning the lab's technology with the company's operational needs, defining the scope of a potential pilot project and outlining the general terms of a future partnership.
