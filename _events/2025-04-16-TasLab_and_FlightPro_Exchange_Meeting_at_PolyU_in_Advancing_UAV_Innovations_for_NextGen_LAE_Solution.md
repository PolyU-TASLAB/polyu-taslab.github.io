In April 2025, the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU) hosted an exchange meeting in Hong Kong with FlightPro, a UAV consultancy and training firm. The two sides explored synergies in talent development, regulatory compliance and real-world application testing. The discussions centred on using FlightPro's industry expertise to support TASLAB's research commercialisation efforts, particularly in Hong Kong's growing low-altitude economy (LAE) ecosystem.
