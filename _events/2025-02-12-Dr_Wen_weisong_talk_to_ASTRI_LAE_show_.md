Dr. Weisong Wen was invited to join a panel discussion at the 2025 ASTRI Tech Applied Summit, held on 10 February at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre. The summit was the city's first major innovation and technology event of the year. It was organised by the Hong Kong Applied Science and Technology Research Institute (ASTRI) with the support of the Hong Kong SAR Government's Innovation and Technology Department, and brought together over 1,500 participants, including leaders from government, industry, academia, research and investment sectors worldwide.

The summit focused on topics such as generative AI, the low-altitude economy (LAE) and smart cities, showcasing Hong Kong's ambition to become a global innovation hub. More details about the summit are available at this [link](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/dBr8Zv8IsPlHgUaKZ_ow9Q).
