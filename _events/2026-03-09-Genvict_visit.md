The Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU) held a strategic cooperation meeting with Shenzhen Genvict Technologies Co., Ltd., a leading end-to-end provider of mobility intelligence solutions specialising in electronic toll collection (ETC), smart urban mobility and cooperative ITS (C-ITS). The meeting explored opportunities for collaboration in smart mobility and the low-altitude economy. The engagement reflects PolyU's commitment to industry–academia partnerships and to practical solutions for next-generation transportation systems that address real-world challenges in urban and highway mobility.

The meeting highlighted joint R&D directions in intelligent transportation, including AI-driven traffic management, robotics-enabled last-mile delivery, sensor-fusion-based perception for C-ITS, and IoT connectivity for real-time fleet and infrastructure optimization. Both parties emphasised deployable pilots for smart mobility and multimodal coordination across agents, micromobility and service vehicles.
