On 12 March, the China Intelligent & Connected Vehicles (Beijing) Research Institute (CICV) visited The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). The meeting with PolyU representatives explored collaboration opportunities in vehicle security, wireless charging, V2X, the low-altitude economy and embedded AI. The visit underscores PolyU's commitment to industry–academia partnerships and to practical solutions for next-generation transportation systems that address real-world challenges.

### About CICV

CICV was jointly initiated by the China Society of Automotive Engineers (China SAE), the China Association of Automobile Manufacturers (CAAM) and the China Industry Innovation Alliance for the Intelligent and Connected Vehicles (CAICV). The company has 10 departments and 153 employees.
