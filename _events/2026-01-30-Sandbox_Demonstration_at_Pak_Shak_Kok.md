On 30 January 2026, the team reached a significant milestone by completing its first cross-sea logistics test flight for Phase 1 of the **Hong Kong Low-altitude Economy Regulatory Sandbox**.

### The test flight

The test flight was carried out with the strong support and on-site witnessing of the Civil Aviation Department (CAD) of Hong Kong and the team's partners. The operation validated the stability and reliability of the logistics solution within the sandbox framework.

### Experience and outlook

The team sincerely thanks the Hong Kong CAD and its partners for their support of its research and operations. The flight provided valuable flight data and operational experience specific to cross-sea logistics, and the team looks forward to contributing further to the development of Hong Kong's low-altitude economy.
