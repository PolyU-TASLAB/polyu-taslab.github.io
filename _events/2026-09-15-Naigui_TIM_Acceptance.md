We are pleased to share that the paper "**Outlier-Aware LiDAR/IMU/UWB Integration with NLOS Mitigation and Anchor Self-Calibration in Complex Scenarios**" has been accepted for publication in the [IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement (TIM)](https://ieee-ims.org/publication/ieee-tim) on 15 September 2026.

The work was led by PhD student **Naigui Xiao** at the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB), The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). The authors include Dr. Lu Bai (Beihang University, a former postdoctoral fellow at TASLAB), Xiangru Wang, Jiahao Hu, Qi Zhang, Dr. Jianhao Jiao, Zhen Lyu, Dr. Feng Huang and Prof. Weisong Wen.

As its title indicates, the paper studies the integration of LiDAR, inertial (IMU) and ultra-wideband (UWB) measurements for positioning in complex scenarios, with attention to measurement outliers, non-line-of-sight (NLOS) UWB signals and the self-calibration of UWB anchors.
