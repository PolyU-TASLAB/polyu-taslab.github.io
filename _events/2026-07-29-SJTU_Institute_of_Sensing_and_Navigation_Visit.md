On 29 July 2026, members of the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) visited the [Institute of Sensing and Navigation at Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://ast.sjtu.edu.cn/web/index) at the invitation of [Prof. Ling Pei](https://ast.sjtu.edu.cn/web/base/personal-info/5e50b16a6b5cc99eeca74884?index=2&child=1). The TASLAB delegation comprised Mr. Ziqi Zhang, Mr. Qi Zhang, Mr. Qijun Qin, Mr. Yingming Chen and Dr. Feng Huang.

The visit included a tour of the institute's laboratory facilities, where the TASLAB delegation learned about its research environment, experimental platforms and ongoing work in sensing and navigation. The tour gave both teams an opportunity to exchange perspectives on their research activities and areas of shared interest.

During the sharing sessions, researchers from TASLAB and the Institute of Sensing and Navigation introduced their recent work and discussed developments in sensing, positioning, navigation and autonomous systems. The exchange encouraged constructive technical dialogue and allowed participants to explore potential directions for future academic collaboration.

The visit strengthened the connection between TASLAB and the Institute of Sensing and Navigation and laid a foundation for continued research exchange between The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU) and Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
