On 14 March 2025, the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU) welcomed Mr. Kenny Lau, Chief Technology Officer of S.F. Express Hong Kong, for a strategic visit. The meeting focused on advancing unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) technologies and accelerating the development of Hong Kong's low-altitude economy (LAE), a sector poised to redefine urban logistics, emergency response and regional connectivity. Dr. Wen Weisong, Administrative Supervisor of TASLAB, organized the discussion with Mr. Lau.

### Discussion topics

- **UAV innovations**: progress in drone delivery systems, autonomous navigation and AI-driven airspace management.
- **Low-altitude economy**: opportunities for Hong Kong to pioneer smart air mobility infrastructure, regulatory frameworks and cross-sector partnerships.
- **Sustainability &amp; safety**: balancing technological growth with environmental and operational safeguard policies.

The discussion gave participants valuable insights and produced meaningful ideas about the industries affected by the low-altitude economy.

As a global logistics hub, Hong Kong is uniquely positioned to lead the low-altitude economy, a growing sector projected to unlock billions in economic value through aerial logistics, surveillance and passenger transport. S.F. Express, a pioneer in drone delivery trials across Greater China, brings critical industry insights, while TASLAB's research in aeronautical systems provides the technical backbone for scalable solutions.
