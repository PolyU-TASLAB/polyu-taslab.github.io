*Hong Kong, 11 September 2025* – The Hong Kong Low Altitude Economy Drone Take-off Reliability Index (DTORI) was officially launched today at a ceremony attended by government officials, industry leaders and academic experts. The initiative, introduced by the Hong Kong Innovative Smart Applications Association (HKISA), aims to standardise and enhance the safety and operational reliability of drones across the city's rapidly expanding low-altitude economy (LAE).

The ceremony featured a keynote address by representatives of HKISA, who emphasised the critical role of the new index in promoting trust and scalability in commercial and civic drone applications. The index will provide a measurable framework to evaluate drone performance, maintenance standards and operational safety, which are key factors for integration into urban airspace.

A highlight of the event was a demonstration by drone technology company DAMODA, which presented a synchronised drone swarm light show. Hundreds of drones took to the skies, forming dynamic and intricate patterns that illustrated both the artistic and functional potential of swarm technology. The display underscored the reliability and precision of drone systems that can support fields such as logistics, emergency response and public entertainment.

Officials praised the collaboration between research institutions, regulatory bodies and private enterprises in positioning Hong Kong as a leader in smart city innovation. The launch marks a significant step towards the safe and efficient integration of drones into daily life and commercial operations.

Looking ahead, HKISA announced plans to expand the index's criteria and to encourage testing cooperation and widespread adoption among drone manufacturers and operators.
