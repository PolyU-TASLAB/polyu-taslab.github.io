Our lab members Dr. HUANG Feng and PhD student ZHONG Yihan are presenting their work at IEEE IROS 2025. After 19 years, IROS has returned to China, at a pivotal moment in the rapid advancement of AI and robotics, making IROS 2025 an excellent venue for discussion and networking. The data of our work is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/DarrenWong/RSG-GLIO).
