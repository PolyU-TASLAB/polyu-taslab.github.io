This page lists my news and events in 2023.

- [**12/30/2023**] We are happy to share that our proposal titled "**Data-driven-assisted GNSS RTK/INS Navigation for Autonomous Systems in Urban Canyons**" was granted by the [PolyU Research Centres](https://rc-dsai.comp.polyu.edu.hk/). This project aims to explore the potential of AI in improving GNSS positioning in urban canyons, in collaboration with [Dr. Li Jing](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/comp/people/academic-staff/dr-li-jing-amelia/) from the PolyU Department of Computing.

- [**12/11/2023**] We are glad to share that our GLIO paper (Liu, X., Wen, W. and Hsu, L.T., 2023. *GLIO: Tightly-Coupled GNSS/LiDAR/IMU Integration for Continuous and Drift-free State Estimation of Intelligent Vehicles in Urban Areas*.
