This month, Our Hong Kong Foundation (OHKF) released its latest research report, "Taking Flight — Forging a Future for Hong Kong's Low-Altitude Economy", an in-depth discussion of the future development of Hong Kong's low-altitude economy (LAE). The Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) was one of the teams supporting the report.

### Team support and key discussion points

As a supporting team for the report, TASLAB held in-depth discussions on the following key issues:
1. The government support needed by university research teams, such as the Civil Aviation Department's review and support for research drone test flights;
2. The critical breakthroughs required to transform low-altitude technology research into industrial applications in Hong Kong;
3. How to strengthen collaboration between academia and industry to bring economic benefits to Hong Kong;
4. How Hong Kong can work in synergy with Mainland China's low-altitude economy industry during its development.

### Academic and industry exchange

TASLAB also invited researchers from OHKF to visit The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU) and take part in several academic and industry sharing and discussion sessions.
