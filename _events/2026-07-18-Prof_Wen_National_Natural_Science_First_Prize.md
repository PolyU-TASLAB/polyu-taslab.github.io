Professor Weisong Wen, Director of The Hong Kong Polytechnic University's Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TAS LAB), has contributed as **Co-Investigator (Co-I)** to a research project that has received the **Natural Science Award (First Prize)** from the **China Simulation Federation**.

The Natural Science Award, conferred by the China Simulation Federation, recognizes outstanding research contributions in simulation science and related fields. The First Prize honors projects with significant scientific merit and impact. Prof. Wen's involvement as Co-I underscores his collaborative research strengths in autonomous systems and related technologies.

TAS LAB warmly congratulates the entire project team on this achievement and is proud of Prof. Wen's role in contributing to this research endeavor.
