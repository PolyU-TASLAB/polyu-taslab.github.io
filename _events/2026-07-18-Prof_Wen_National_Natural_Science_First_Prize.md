Prof. Weisong Wen, Director of the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), has contributed as **Co-Investigator (Co-I)** to a research project that has received the **Natural Science Award (First Prize)** from the **China Simulation Federation**.

<p class="news-caption">Natural Science Award (First Prize) from the China Simulation Federation: the proposed awardee list, with Prof. Weisong Wen listed as Co-Investigator.</p>

The Natural Science Award, conferred by the China Simulation Federation, recognises outstanding research contributions in simulation science and related fields. The First Prize honours projects with significant scientific merit and impact. Prof. Wen's involvement as Co-I reflects his collaborative research strengths in autonomous systems and related technologies.

TASLAB congratulates the entire project team on this achievement and Prof. Wen on his contribution to the research.
