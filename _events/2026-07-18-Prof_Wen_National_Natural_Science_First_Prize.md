Professor Weisong Wen, Director of The Hong Kong Polytechnic University's Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TAS LAB), has contributed as **Co-Investigator (Co-I)** to a research project that has been honored with the prestigious **National Natural Science Award (First Prize)**, one of China's highest recognitions in fundamental research.

The National Natural Science Award, conferred by the State Council of the People's Republic of China, recognizes outstanding achievements in basic and applied basic research. The First Prize is awarded to projects demonstrating groundbreaking scientific contributions with profound impact on the research community. Prof. Wen's involvement as Co-I underscores his collaborative research strengths in autonomous systems and related technologies.

TAS LAB warmly congratulates the entire project team on this outstanding achievement and is proud of Prof. Wen's role in contributing to this nationally recognized research endeavor. The laboratory remains committed to high-impact collaborative research that advances the frontiers of trustworthy AI and autonomous systems.

Learn more about the [National Natural Science Award](https://www.nsfc.gov.cn/).
