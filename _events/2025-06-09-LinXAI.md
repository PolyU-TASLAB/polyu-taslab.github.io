On 9 June 2025, Dr. Weisong Wen, Principal Investigator (PI) of the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB), together with postdoctoral researchers Dr. Runqiu Yang and Dr. Yingying Wang and research assistant Xiangru Wang, visited LinxAI Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. in Shenzhen to explore advancements in quadruped robotics. The team toured the company's quadruped robot assembly facility and R&D lab, gaining insights into its robotic technologies and industrial applications.

During the visit, the researchers held in-depth discussions with LinxAI's CEO and engineering team, exchanging ideas on robotics innovation, AI integration and the future development of the joint lab. The visit strengthened the ties between academia and industry and paves the way for future partnerships in intelligent robotics.

The delegation expressed enthusiasm about LinxAI's technological achievements and looks forward to further interdisciplinary cooperation in robotics and AI-driven automation.
