This page lists my news and events in 2018.

- [**11/09/2018**] On 9 Nov 2018, I took part in the 2018 **Bay Area Robotics Symposium at Stanford University**, which aims to bring together roboticists from the Bay Area. The program consisted of a mix of faculty, student, and industry presentations.

- [**11/09/2018**] On 9 Nov 2018, I gave a talk at **Deepmap.ai with Mark Wheeler and Lionel Garin**. The topic was "Localization for Autonomous Vehicles in Dense Urban". Mark Wheeler previously worked at Google Maps for about 8 years. Lionel Garin is a worldwide-recognized GNSS expert.

- [**12/02/2018**] On 2 Dec 2018, I gave a presentation at the [8th East Asia Mechanical Aerospace Engineering Workshop](https://research.polyu.edu.hk/en/activities/8th-east-asia-mechanical-and-aerospace-engineering-workshop) with Dr. Peng and Ankit Ravankar. The topic was "Multi-sensor Integration-based Localization for Autonomous Vehicle in Urbanized Area".

- [**12/07/2018**] On 7 Dec 2018, I gave a talk at the Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, with [Prof. Huiyun Li](http://english.siat.cas.cn/SI2017/IAIT2017/RC1/CAE/Researchers1/201707/t20170719_180000.html). The topic was **"Multi-sensor Integration-based Localization for Autonomous Vehicle in Urbanized Area"**.

- [**08/28/2018**] On 28 Aug 2018, I had a remote talk with the team at Tencent. The topic was **multi-sensor fusion for autonomous driving**.

- [**08/27/2018**] On 27 Aug 2018, I had a remote talk with the team at Nagoya University with [Yuki Kitsukawa](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Yuki-Kitsukawa), who is the CEO of Map IV. The topic was mapping and localization for autonomous driving.

- [**08/17/2018**] On 17 Aug 2018, I visited the autonomous driving team at Jingchi in the U.S.

- [**08/16/2018**] On 16 Aug 2018, I gave a talk at Baidu Inc. in the USA. The topic was **GNSS positioning aided by LiDAR and building models**.

- [**07/18/2018**] On 18 Jul 2018, I visited the [MSC Lab](https://msc.berkeley.edu/) at the University of California, Berkeley, to do research on multi-sensor fusion aided by perception.
