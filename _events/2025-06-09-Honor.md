Shenzhen, 9 June 2025 – Dr. Weisong Wen, Principal Investigator (PI) of the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB), recently visited Honor Device Co., Ltd. in Shenzhen together with postdoctoral researchers Dr. Shiyu Bai, Dr. Runqiu Yang and Dr. Yingying Wang, and research assistant Xiangru Wang. The visit focused on the continuity and future direction of the AI-driven pedestrian dead reckoning (AI-PDR) project.

During the visit, the researchers held in-depth discussions with Honor's director and engineering team on strategies to sustain and advance the AI-PDR initiative. The exchange of ideas on AI-PDR and robotics innovation gave both sides a deeper understanding of industry needs and research challenges. The collaboration aims to ensure the long-term viability and impact of the AI-PDR project and to strengthen ties between academia and industry for future partnerships in intelligent systems.
