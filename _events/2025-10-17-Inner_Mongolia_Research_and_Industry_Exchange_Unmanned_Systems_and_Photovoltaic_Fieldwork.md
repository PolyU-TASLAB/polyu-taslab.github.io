In late October, members of the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) visited several key institutions and enterprises in Inner Mongolia to discuss the application of unmanned systems, and carried out drone-based photovoltaic cleaning and data collection at a desert power station.

### Visits to key institutions and enterprises

The team visited the following organisations:

- **Ordos Institute of Applied Technology**
- **Ordos Modern Industry Institute**
- **Inner Mongolia Huiju High-Tech Co., Ltd.**
- **Inner Mongolia Kubuqi Desert Photovoltaic Energy Co., Ltd.**

At one of the power stations of Inner Mongolia Kubuqi Desert Photovoltaic Energy Co., Ltd., the team carried out practical drone-based photovoltaic cleaning and data collection. This work lays a solid foundation for future research on unmanned systems.

TASLAB looks forward to further advancing unmanned system technologies and strengthening cooperation between academia and industry in Inner Mongolia.
