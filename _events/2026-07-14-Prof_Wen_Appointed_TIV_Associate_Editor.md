Professor Weisong Wen, Director of The Hong Kong Polytechnic University's Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TAS LAB), has officially been appointed an **Associate Editor of the IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles (T-IV)**.

Published by the IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society, T-IV provides an international forum for research and innovation in intelligent vehicles and related technologies. In his new editorial role, Prof. Wen will support the journal's peer-review process and contribute to maintaining the quality and impact of research published in the field.

The appointment recognizes Prof. Wen's expertise and contributions in trustworthy autonomous systems, intelligent vehicles, multi-sensor fusion, and robust positioning and navigation. TAS LAB warmly congratulates Prof. Wen on joining the T-IV editorial team and looks forward to his continued service to the international intelligent transportation and autonomous systems research communities.

Learn more about [IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles](https://ieee-itss.org/pub/t-iv/).
