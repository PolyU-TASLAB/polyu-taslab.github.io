Professor Weisong Wen, Director of the Trustworthy AI and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASLAB) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), has been officially appointed an **Associate Editor of the IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles (T-IV)**.

Published by the IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society, T-IV provides an international forum for research and innovation in intelligent vehicles and related technologies. In his new editorial role, Prof. Wen will support the journal's peer-review process and help maintain the quality and impact of research published in the field.

The appointment recognises Prof. Wen's expertise and contributions in trustworthy autonomous systems, intelligent vehicles, multi-sensor fusion, and robust positioning and navigation. TASLAB warmly congratulates Prof. Wen on joining the T-IV editorial team and looks forward to his continued service to the international intelligent transportation and autonomous systems research communities.

Learn more about [IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles](https://ieee-itss.org/pub/t-iv/).
