Panpan Zhang received a PhD in Mechatronic Engineering from Zhejiang University in 2026. She received a BEng in Mechanical Engineering from Jilin University in 2018. She is a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Her research focuses on the development of artificial intelligence algorithms for perception, navigation, and control of autonomous systems.

**Research Areas**

Intelligent Control; Reinforcement Learning; SLAM; Robotics
