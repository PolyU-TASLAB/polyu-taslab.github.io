Bing Han received the bachelor’s degree in Electronic Information Engineering (Science Experimental Class) from Hainan University, Haikou, China, in 2023. In the same year, she joined the School of Information and Electronics, Beijing Institute of Technology, Beijing, China, to pursue the master’s degree. In 2025, she transferred to the School of Automation, Beijing Institute of Technology, to pursue the Ph.D. degree. She is currently pursuing dual Ph.D. degrees with the School of Automation, Beijing Institute of Technology, Beijing, China, and the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Hong Kong.

**Research Areas**

Visual SLAM; Multi-sensor Fusion Localization; Robust Perception and Localization; Learning-based and Differentiable SLAM
