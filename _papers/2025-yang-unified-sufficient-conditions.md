## Problem Addressed

Exact convex relaxation of general nonconvex optimal control problems remains difficult, with existing sufficient conditions restricted to particular problem structures and not directly applicable to broader formulations.

## Key Contributions

- Two unified sufficient conditions guaranteeing exactness of convex relaxation for general nonconvex optimal control problems
- Novel proof technique based on a locally constructed subproblem
- Transformation into classical controllability, normality, and strong observability conditions for specific constraint set forms
- Approximation methods modifying cost function, control constraints, and system dynamics to satisfy the conditions

## Conclusions

The proposed conditions unify and extend prior exact convex relaxation theory, and the approximation methods ensure the sufficient conditions hold in specific scenarios. Applied to Mars landing problems, the theoretical results are confirmed numerically, demonstrating that under glide-slope constraints the nonconvex problem can be converted into an exactly equivalent convex one.
